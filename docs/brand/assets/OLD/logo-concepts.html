Logo concepts

Five directions, each shown on the site's dark ground and on white, with the header lockup and favicon sizes that will do most of the real work. The brand facts they draw from: the Capitol dome (the subject), candlesticks (the data), and the disclosure clock (the speed story your latency scoreboard already tells).

01

Candlestick Colonnade

02

Capitol Skyline sparkline

One continuous price line that rises from the left, traces the whole Capitol elevation — wing wall, wing roof, center block, dome with lantern tick, back down the far wing — and exits still trading. The mark is a chart; the chart is the building. Naturally horizontal — strongest as a header/social lockup.

Congress.Trade
  • Motion built in — draws itself beautifully as a loading animation or hero reveal (the line literally "discloses" the dome).
  • Ties directly to the latency-race story: a price line arriving somewhere first.
  • Wide aspect ratio squares badly — the favicon crop loses the entry/exit tails that make it a chart.
03

C·T Ticker Monogram

The domain's own punctuation as the mark: C and T set like a ticker symbol, the period drawn as a single green candle. Reads "CT" at any size and puts the .Trade TLD — the most distinctive part of the name — at the center of the identity.

04

Disclosure Stopwatch

A stopwatch whose face holds the Capitol — dome, entablature, colonnade, and base, not just the dome — with a rising hand: the 45-day disclosure clock, and the fact that you beat FMP on 22 of 23 matched filings. Rev 2 adds the fuller building and two red/white/blue studies — the patriotic palette suits this badge (it's a civic *event* mark, and R/W/B here can't be mistaken for buy/sell the way it could on the colonnade's candles).

left: site accent · middle: R/W/B on dark (white case, blue Capitol, red hand) · right: R/W/B on white (navy + red)
  • Owns the speed claim — no competitor leads with "we're first," and this badge says only that.
  • Perfect stamp for the scoreboard, alert emails, and the pricing page's proof strip.
  • As the everything-logo it under-tells the data story; better as the latency sub-mark next to concept 01.
