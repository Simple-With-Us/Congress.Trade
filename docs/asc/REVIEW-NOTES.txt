What the app is
Congress.Trade publishes stock-trade disclosures that U.S. House and Senate members, and executive-branch officials, file publicly under the STOCK Act (2012).  All underlying data comes from public government records.  Dollar figures are estimates derived from disclosure brackets.  It is an educational and informational tool, not investment advice, and is not affiliated with the U.S. Congress or any government agency.
