# 2026-08-19 — OGE executive poll interval is 15 minutes

**Superseded the same day** by
`2026-08-19-oge-adaptive-probe-schedule.md`: executive now uses the
House/Senate adaptive `probeSchedule` / `decideSourcePoll` path.  The
15-minute figure remains only as the weekday coverage floor, not a flat
timer.  `OGE_POLL_INTERVAL_SEC` is unused.

## Summary

The OGE executive watcher defaulted to 6 hours (`DEFAULT_POLL_INTERVAL_SEC = 21600`) as a politeness / "filings are rare" cadence.  That loses to other sources.  The product default is now **15 minutes (900 seconds)** so we beat other sources to new OGE Form 278-T filings.

After success wait 15 min.  After failure wait 15 min too (`OGE_FAILURE_BACKOFF_SEC = 900`).  `last_poll:oge` only advances on success, so without a backoff a down OGE would be hit every minutely cron tick.  Matching the success interval means a down OGE is not polled more often than a healthy one (a 10 min backoff would have been).  House and Senate adaptive probe schedules are unchanged.

`pollIntervalSec` still reads Infisical first, then the env var, then this default.  A leftover Infisical / Coolify `OGE_POLL_INTERVAL_SEC=21600` keeps the old 6h cadence after deploy.  This repo does not write Infisical.  Set the live CT prod secret to `900`.
