# 2026-10-16 — House Live-Search Overlay Root Cause & Default-Off Fix

## Summary

Investigation of all 23 House STOCK Act PTR filings ingested between October 2 and October 16, 2026 (plus all 30 House discovery batches in `ingest_log` across 108 days of production history) established why `pollHouseLiveSearch()` (`POST https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewMemberSearchResult`) never discovers filings intraday ahead of the daily bulk XML index (`https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/public_disc/financial-pdfs/{YEAR}FD.ZIP`):

1. **Upstream Clerk architecture (shared daily ~09:00 ET / 13:00 UTC publication batch):**
   - The House Clerk's interactive member search (`POST /FinancialDisclosure/ViewMemberSearchResult`) and the yearly bulk XML (`{YEAR}FD.ZIP` -> `{YEAR}FD.xml`) refresh on the exact same once-daily publication cycle at ~09:00 ET (`13:00 UTC` during EDT) on business days.  Neither endpoint surfaces filings intraday after 09:00 ET.
   - Live comparison on `2026-10-16`: `2026FD.ZIP` (`Last-Modified: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:00:04 GMT`) and `POST /FinancialDisclosure/ViewMemberSearchResult` (`FilingYear=2026`) both return the exact same **427** PTR `DocID`s (`live - bulk = []`, `bulk - live = []`).
   - HTTP `Last-Modified` headers on the 23 October 2026 PTR PDFs (`/public_disc/ptr-pdfs/2026/{docId}.pdf`) show the exact UTC timestamp each PDF was signed/generated by the Clerk's e-filing system:
     - On October 14, 2026, `20035487` (Daniel Meuser) was signed at `12:49:54 GMT` (`08:49:54 EDT`, 10 minutes before the `09:00 EDT` daily cut) and published in the October 14 `13:00 GMT` batch (`first_seen_at = 2026-10-14T13:02:00.001Z`, 12 minutes after PDF creation).
     - Four other October 14 filings — `20035627` (Suzan K. DelBene, PDF `Last-Modified: 19:36:44 GMT`), `20035648` (Richard W. Allen, `20:40:53 GMT`), `20035651` (Rich McCormick, `20:46:54 GMT`), and `20035652` (Robin Kelly, `21:04:57 GMT`) — were signed in the afternoon after `13:00 GMT`.  Between `19:36:44Z` and `2026-10-15T13:00:00Z`, `source_attempts` recorded **45 consecutive successful House polls**; all 45 returned `0` new filings because the Clerk holds post-09:00 ET signings until the next business morning's `09:00 ET` (`13:00 UTC`) batch.  All four appeared simultaneously when the Clerk's index refreshed at `Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:00:04 GMT`.
     - Because `<FilingDate>` is a date-only string (`YYYY-MM-DD`, stored as `00:00:00Z`), an afternoon signing held until `13:00 UTC` the next day measures as `24 + 13 = 37h` from midnight UTC of `filed_date` (and Friday afternoon signings `20035558` / `20035553` on Oct 2 held until Monday Oct 5 `13:00 UTC` measure as `48 + 13 = 61h`).

2. **Client & timeout defects in `pollHouseLiveSearch()` and `pollHouse()`:**
   - **Fake DataTables pagination loop caused 2x redundant 207 KB POSTs per poll:** `ViewMemberSearchResult` is a server-rendered Razor HTML view (`<table class="library-table table" role="grid" data-paging="true">`) where client-side DataTables (`main.min.js`) paginates the rendered DOM in the browser.  The server ignores `FilingType=P`, `draw=1`, `start`, and `length`, returning all 436 rows (`207,505` bytes, 427 PTRs) in every response.  Because `427 >= limit (100)`, `pollHouseLiveSearch()` always slept 500ms and issued a second identical 207 KB POST (`offset=100`) before `newItems === 0` exited the loop.
   - **Per-source 7s timeout in `pollHouseLiveSearch()` discarded already-fetched bulk XML filings:** `pollHouse()` shares a single 7-second `WATCHER_SOURCE_IO_BUDGET_MS` across `fetchHouseIndex()` and `pollHouseLiveSearch()`.  When `fetchHouseIndex()` succeeded and the subsequent 3-request `pollHouseLiveSearch()` timed out, `if (signal?.aborted) throw err;` rethrew the per-source `TimeoutError`, discarding the already-fetched bulk XML rows, recording a `source_attempts` `failure` (`The operation was aborted due to timeout`, observed on `2026-10-15T19:37:00Z`), and triggering a 10-minute `lastAttemptFailed` backoff (delaying the Oct 15 `13:00:04Z` batch discovery from `13:01Z` to `13:12Z`).
   - **Outbound header leak:** `pollHouseLiveSearch()` attached `x-residential-proxy-url` directly to outbound requests sent to `disclosures-clerk.house.gov`.

### What Changed

- **`app/src/ingestion/watcher.ts`:**
  - Changed `houseLiveSearchEnabled(env)` from default-on (`!== 'false'`) to opt-in (`=== 'true'`) so `pollHouse()` only polls `{YEAR}FD.ZIP` by default unless `HOUSE_LIVE_SEARCH_ENABLED=true` is explicitly configured.
  - Threaded the enclosing watcher `tickSignal` (`options.signal`) into `pollHouse()` separately from the 7-second per-source `signal` so a per-source timeout inside the optional prior-year sweep or live-search overlay fails soft instead of discarding already-fetched current-year bulk XML filings.
- **`app/src/ingestion/houseSource.ts`:**
  - Removed the fake DataTables `while (true)` offset loop in `pollHouseLiveSearch()` so it performs a single `GET /ViewSearch` + `POST /ViewMemberSearchResult` per poll.
  - Simplified `buildHouseSearchBody(year)` to send only the form fields read by the Razor view (`LastName`, `FilingYear`, `State`, `District`).
  - Removed the leaked `x-residential-proxy-url` request header from `customHeaders` and `postHeaders`.
- **`app/src/shared/types.ts`:**
  - Updated `HOUSE_LIVE_SEARCH_ENABLED` JSDoc to reflect opt-in (`=== 'true'`) semantics.
- **`app/src/ingestion/__tests__/houseSource.test.ts` & `app/src/ingestion/__tests__/watcher.test.ts`:**
  - Added regression tests covering single-POST unpaginated HTML responses (`>100` PTR rows), header hygiene, default-off `HOUSE_LIVE_SEARCH_ENABLED`, and bulk-index preservation when `pollHouseLiveSearch()` hits a per-source `TimeoutError`.
