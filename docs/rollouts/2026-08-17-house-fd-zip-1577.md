# 2026-08-17 — House bulk FD ZIP health (#1577)

## Summary

Issue #1577 asked whether the House Clerk yearly FD ZIP was silently
degraded, leaving 186 persisted filings `filed_date`-NULL past the 72h
catch-up window.  Live check on 2026-08-17: the ZIP is reachable and
complete.  Official persisted House rows already have dates.  Remaining
NULLs are ids the Clerk index does not contain, so NULL is honest.
Do not invent dates.

## Ask, answered

1. **Is `https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/public_disc/financial-pdfs/{YEAR}FD.ZIP` reachable and complete?**
   Yes.  Direct GET with the production UA (`congress-feed/0.1`) returned
   HTTP 200, `application/x-zip-compressed`, valid `PK` zip, and a full
   `{YEAR}FD.xml` for 2024 / 2025 / 2026.

   | Year | Bytes | Last-Modified (GET) | Members with DocID | PTRs | PTR FilingDate empty |
   |------|------:|---------------------|-------------------:|-----:|---------------------:|
   | 2024 | 81,465 | Fri, 14 Aug 2026 13:00:53 GMT | 2,286 | 451 | 0 |
   | 2025 | 104,740 | Fri, 14 Aug 2026 13:00:53 GMT | 2,899 | 515 | 0 |
   | 2026 | 55,996 | Mon, 17 Aug 2026 13:00:12 GMT | 1,553 | 353 | 0 |

   2026 rebuilt the morning of this check (not a stale cache).  Filing
   dates in that index run through 2026-08-15.  The 34 undated 2026
   members are all type `W` (not PTRs).  XML is well-formed; no missing
   DocID rows.  A HEAD/GET size mismatch on 2024 (HEAD advertised a
   newer 81,531-byte object) did not affect GET: both UAs downloaded
   the same complete 2,286-member archive.

2. **Are the stuck `doc_id`s in that index?**
   Prod (`POST /api/admin/debug-sql`, 2026-08-17 ~21:08Z, live sha
   `8965f758`):

   - House `filed_date IS NULL`: 971 rows.
   - Official `H-YYYY-*` persisted with NULL or empty `filed_date`: **0**.
   - Official `H-2026-*` persisted: 317.  All 317 are present in today's
     2026 ZIP (spot-check `H-2026-9116249` Cole / `H-2026-9116256`
     Harshbarger both carry FilingDate).
   - Official `H-YYYY-*` still NULL: 881 rows, all `ingest_status='not_found'`,
     all in the 2026-07-30 frontier-probe range `H-2026-20035076` …
     `H-2026-20035975`.  **0 of 881** appear in the 2026 (or 2025/2024)
     Clerk index.
   - Persisted NULL remaining: 90 rows, every one
     `provider-missing-quiver-house-quiver-*` (first_seen
     2026-07-27T18:54:44Z).  Those ids are third-party stubs, not Clerk
     DocIDs, so they cannot appear in the ZIP.

   The Aug 9 count of 186 persisted NULLs is therefore a healed
   official-House leftover plus these provider stubs.  Official
   live-search rows picked up dates from later ZIP polls / the hourly
   sweep.  The stubs and the 404-probe phantoms never will.

3. **Does `sweepFiledDateBackfill`'s `H-(\d{4})-` regex miss a subset?**
   No.  Every official House pipeline id is `H-{year}-{DocID}`
   (`houseDocId` in `houseSource.ts`).  The 90 remaining persisted NULLs
   fail that regex on purpose (`provider-missing-%`, already excluded).
   Loosening the regex would not find a Clerk date for them.

## Code change

`sweepFiledDateBackfill` now also skips `ingest_status='not_found'`.
Those rows are the documented 2026-07-30 sequential-probe burst
(detectionRoutes.ts HEAD-404 fix).  They match `H-(\d{4})-`, so the
hourly sweep was re-fetching 2026FD.ZIP forever and updating nothing.
Skipping them does not invent dates; it stops a no-op Clerk fetch.
